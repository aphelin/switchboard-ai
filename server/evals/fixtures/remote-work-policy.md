# Acme Cloud — Remote and Hybrid Work Policy (HR-POL-014, effective 1 March 2026)

## Scope

This policy applies to all Acme Cloud employees except data-centre technicians, whose roles require on-site presence.

## Working models

Employees choose one of three models each quarter:

1. **Office-first** — at least 4 days per week in the office.
2. **Hybrid** — at least 2 days per week in the office; the team agrees on a shared anchor day (Tuesday for Engineering, Wednesday for Sales).
3. **Remote** — no office requirement. Remote employees must live in a country where Acme Cloud has a legal entity: Estonia, Germany, Norway or Poland.

Changes to the working model are requested in the HR portal before the 15th of the last month of the quarter and take effect at the start of the next quarter.

## Equipment and allowances

- Every remote or hybrid employee receives a one-time home-office allowance of **€600** for furniture and equipment, paid with the first salary after approval.
- A monthly internet and utilities allowance of **€40** is paid to remote employees only.
- Laptops and monitors remain company property and must be returned within 10 days of the last working day.

## Working hours and availability

Core hours are **10:00–15:00 CET**, during which everyone must be reachable on Slack. Outside core hours employees may schedule their day freely, as long as the contractual 40 hours per week are met.

Employees may work from another country for up to **30 calendar days per year** (the "workation" allowance). Workations longer than 14 consecutive days require written approval from the line manager and HR because of tax and social-security rules.

## Security requirements

- Only company-managed laptops may access production systems.
- Public Wi-Fi may be used only with the corporate VPN enabled.
- Screens must be locked when unattended; confidential documents may not be printed at home.

## Meetings

Meetings with at least one remote participant are held as video calls, and a written summary is posted in the team channel within one working day. Recurring team meetings are scheduled inside core hours.

## Policy owner

The policy is owned by the People team (people@acmecloud.example) and is reviewed every 12 months. Questions about tax implications of workations go to the Finance team.
