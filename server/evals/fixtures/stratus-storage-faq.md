# Stratus Storage — Customer FAQ (partner product sheet, v1.4)

## About Stratus

Stratus Storage is a fictional object storage reseller founded in 2021 in Vilnius, Lithuania. It resells capacity from three regions: **Warsaw (WAW-1)**, **Helsinki (HEL-3)** and **Dublin (DUB-2)**. A fourth region in Madrid opened in January 2026.

## Plans and support

| Plan     | Monthly price | Support SLA                 | Included storage |
| -------- | ------------- | --------------------------- | ---------------- |
| Basic    | €19           | Email only, 3 business days | 500 GB           |
| Business | €189          | 8 business hours            | 10 TB            |
| Premium  | €990          | 2 hours, 24/7               | 100 TB           |

Premium customers get a quarterly architecture review with a Stratus solutions engineer. Every plan includes 2 TB of monthly egress; extra egress is billed at €0.035 per GB.

## Durability and backups

Stratus replicates every object three times inside a region and offers 9 nines of durability. Bucket snapshots are taken once per hour and kept for 14 days on Business and 90 days on Premium.

## Security

Stratus is certified to ISO 27017 and encrypts data at rest with AES-256-GCM. Customer-managed keys require the Premium plan. Stratus does not offer a SOC 2 report.

## Billing

Invoices are issued on the 5th of each month and are due within 14 days. Stratus accepts credit cards and PayPal only. Annual prepayment gives a 12% discount.

Tickets are opened at help.stratus-storage.example. Phone support (+370 5 214 0000) is available to Premium customers on weekdays between 09:00 and 17:00 EET.
