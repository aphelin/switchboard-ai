# Acme Cloud — Business Travel and Expense Policy (HR-POL-022, effective 1 January 2026)

## Scope

This policy covers business trips and work-related expenses for all Acme Cloud employees and contractors with a signed travel agreement. It does not cover commuting between home and the office.

## Booking travel

- All flights, trains and hotels are booked through the TravelDesk portal at least **10 working days** before departure.
- Economy class is the default. Business class is allowed for flights longer than **6 hours** of scheduled flight time.
- Trains are preferred over flights for journeys under **4 hours** door to door.

## Per diem and meals

Employees receive a daily meal allowance (per diem) while travelling:

| Destination                   | Per diem |
| ----------------------------- | -------- |
| Estonia, Poland               | €35      |
| Germany, Norway               | €55      |
| United States, United Kingdom | €70      |

The per diem is reduced by 50% on days when breakfast and lunch are provided by a conference or client.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel cap is **€160** in most cities and **€240** in Oslo, London and New York. Stays above the cap need approval from the budget owner before booking.

## Expense claims

Expenses are claimed in Expensify within **30 days** of the end of the trip, with an itemised receipt for anything above **€25**. Claims submitted later than 90 days after the trip are not reimbursed. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary.

## Non-reimbursable items

Minibar charges, fines (parking or traffic), personal travel insurance upgrades and lounge access are never reimbursed.

## Corporate cards

Employees who travel more than six times per year may request a corporate credit card from Finance. Corporate cards have a monthly limit of **€5,000** and must never be used for personal purchases.

## Policy owner

The policy is owned by the Finance team (finance@acmecloud.example) and reviewed every 12 months.
